FAQ

How does this word counter count words?

Any run of characters separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks counts as one word — the same rule used by most editors. Hyphenated terms like "well-known" count as one word; numbers and emoji-only tokens also count.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time uses the widely cited average of 225 words per minute for silent adult reading. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a comfortable presentation pace — handy for speeches, podcasts and video scripts.

What is keyword density and why does it matter for SEO?

Keyword density is how often a word appears relative to your total word count. Repeating your main keyword naturally helps search engines understand the topic, but unnatural stuffing can hurt rankings — the top-10 list helps you keep it balanced.

What are the character limits for Twitter, Instagram and Google?

Twitter/X allows 280 characters per post, Instagram captions allow 2,200, Google typically shows about 160 characters of a meta description and about 60 of a title tag. The progress bars track all four live and turn red when you go over.

Is my text stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens locally in your browser with JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded, stored or logged — close the tab and it is gone.
